# Service illustrations

Existing JPEGs were provided earlier for this application. They are retained.

`home-cleaning.jpg` and `general-cleaning.jpg` were generated with OpenAI ImageGen on 6 November 2026, then resized and encoded as 960 × 640 JPEGs for the app. These are illustrative synthetic people, not photographs of verified staff. They belong on service cards, not professional profiles.

Prompts used (condensed):

- Home cleaning: documentary-style realistic service photograph of an adult Nepali woman in a navy work shirt and rubber gloves wiping a kitchen counter in a bright, modest Kathmandu home; natural daylight, warm neutral tones, candid working posture, no text, logos or watermarks; landscape composition matching the existing worker photos.
- General cleaning: documentary-style realistic service photograph of an adult Nepali man in a navy uniform and gloves mopping a modest Kathmandu living room; natural daylight, warm neutral tones, candid working posture, no text, logos or watermarks; landscape composition matching the existing worker photos.

Full generated PNG originals remain in the Codex generated-images directory. The application bundles only the compressed JPEGs.
